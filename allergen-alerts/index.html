    What Allergen Alerts are

    Group Order diners can save food allergies to their HUNGRY profile. When an order has one, we flag it and ask you to confirm. Orders without alerts work as usual.

    Every order with alerts

    How to confirm

    Use the link in your cutoff email, or go to Allergen Alerts in your Chef Dashboard. Review the flagged items, choose whether you can accommodate them, then click Submit Response.

    Respond quickly

    Reminders and deadlines

    Respond as soon as you get the cutoff email. If we don't hear from you, we'll send a reminder, then a final reminder. After that, our team will contact you.

    Share with your kitchen

    Where you'll see alerts

    Your cutoff email, the Kitchen Prep Doc, labels, the order manifest, your Chef Dashboard and the PDF order summary.
